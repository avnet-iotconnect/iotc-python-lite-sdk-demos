Avnet, Inc. is an American technology distributor headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona. It is one of the oldest and largest distributors of electronic components and technology solutions in the world.

Avnet was founded in 1921 by Charles Avnet in New York City. It began as a small business selling radio parts, on and around Manhattan's Radio Row, and grew alongside the entire electronics industry.

Having been founded in 1921, Avnet has operated for more than a century, making it one of the longest-running companies in the electronics distribution business.

Avnet is a Fortune 500 company and trades on the NASDAQ stock exchange under the ticker symbol AVT.

At its core, Avnet is a distributor: it buys electronic components and products from manufacturers and sells them to companies that build electronic devices, handling logistics, inventory, and supply-chain services.

Beyond simple distribution, Avnet provides design, engineering, supply-chain, and integration services, helping customers take a product from idea through prototype to full production and delivery.

Avnet partners with major semiconductor and technology manufacturers, including NXP, Renesas, Microchip, and Qualcomm, distributing their chips and building reference designs and solutions around them.

In 2016 Avnet acquired Premier Farnell, the distribution group that includes the Farnell and element14 businesses, expanding Avnet's reach with engineers and smaller-volume customers.

Avnet owns element14, an online community and distribution brand for engineers, and Hackster.io, a large community website where hardware developers share projects and learn about new technology.

Avnet Silica and Avnet Abacus are regional distribution arms serving the European market with semiconductors and interconnect, passive, and electromechanical components.

Avnet /IOTCONNECT is Avnet's Internet of Things platform-as-a-service, which helps customers connect, manage, and build applications for fleets of devices without building the cloud infrastructure themselves.

Avnet often demonstrates /IOTCONNECT on development boards from its manufacturer partners, showing how a device can be onboarded, send telemetry, receive commands, and be managed at scale.

Avnet's business model connects the two ends of the electronics industry: the manufacturers who make components and the companies who design and build finished products, adding services and support in between.

Avnet serves customers ranging from large original equipment manufacturers to individual makers and startups, offering both high-volume production supply and small-quantity prototyping parts.

The company operates globally, with distribution centers, warehouses, and offices around the world to move components quickly across international supply chains.

Avnet's long history means it has adapted through many technology eras, from vacuum tubes and radio parts to transistors, integrated circuits, computers, and now connected and AI-enabled devices.

By combining component distribution with cloud services like /IOTCONNECT and communities like Hackster.io, Avnet aims to support the entire journey of building a connected product.

Supply-chain management is a central Avnet service: helping customers forecast demand, manage inventory, and keep production lines supplied with the right parts at the right time.

Avnet's partnerships with semiconductor makers let it offer complete solutions, pairing a manufacturer's silicon, such as an NXP processor, with software, connectivity, and cloud management.

As a distributor with over a hundred years of history, Avnet positions itself as a partner that guides customers through technology choices, from selecting components to deploying and managing devices in the field.
